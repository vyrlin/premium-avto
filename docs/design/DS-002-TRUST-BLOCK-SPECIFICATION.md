# Premium Avto Design System

# DS-002 — Trust Block Specification

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Project Phase:** Product Modernization

---

# 1. Purpose

The Trust Block is the second key component of the public homepage.

Its purpose is not to advertise services.

Its purpose is to explain why owners of VAG vehicles trust Premium Avto.

While the Hero communicates positioning, the Trust Block provides credibility.

---

# 2. Communication Goal

After viewing the Hero, the visitor should already understand:

- where they are;
- what vehicles are serviced.

The Trust Block answers the next question:

> **Why should I trust Premium Avto with my vehicle?**

---

# 3. Core Message

The Trust Block communicates one idea:

> **Expertise built through deep specialization, engineering competence and many years of practical experience with VAG vehicles.**

Every element of the block should reinforce this message.

---

# 4. Layout

The component consists of three logical parts.

## Section Header

Large title.

Recommended wording:

> **Why VAG owners trust Premium Avto**

Below the title is a short introductory paragraph explaining the company's philosophy.

---

## Featured Card

The first card is visually larger than all others.

Its purpose is to communicate long-term expertise.

Recommended content:

- operating since 2006;
- specialists with more than 30 years of VAG experience;
- hundreds of successfully completed engine overhauls;
- engineering approach to every repair.

This card forms the visual center of the component.

---

## Supporting Cards

Four supporting cards surround the featured card.

### Expert Diagnostics

Recommended wording:

> **We successfully solve faults that other repair shops were unable to resolve.**

---

### Correct Spare Parts

Recommended wording:

> **We recommend not the most expensive and not the cheapest parts, but the right parts for each specific repair.**

---

### VAG Specialization

Primary brands:

- Audi
- Volkswagen
- Škoda
- SEAT

Additional brands by agreement:

- Porsche
- Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles

---

### Guaranteed Quality

Communicates:

- engineering repair methods;
- responsibility;
- warranty on completed work.

---

# 5. Visual Style

The Trust Block continues the visual language established by the Hero.

Design principles:

- generous white space;
- premium appearance;
- clean typography;
- subtle shadows;
- restrained use of red accents;
- soft border radius;
- calm visual hierarchy.

The block should feel closer to Audi or Porsche service centers than to traditional repair workshops.

---

# 6. Icons

Icons should reinforce understanding rather than decorate the layout.

Preferred subjects:

- diagnostics;
- engineering;
- warranty;
- spare parts;
- VAG specialization.

Icons should use a consistent visual language.

---

# 7. Responsive Behaviour

Desktop and mobile layouts may differ.

However, both layouts must preserve the same communication hierarchy.

On mobile:

- title;
- introduction;
- featured card;
- supporting cards stacked vertically.

Horizontal scrolling is not permitted.

---

# 8. Relationship with ADR-002

This component is the first practical implementation of:

ADR-002 — Product Positioning.

Every design decision should reinforce the approved positioning of Premium Avto as an independent specialized technical center for VAG vehicles.

---

# 9. Future Evolution

Future improvements may include:

- animations;
- richer illustrations;
- interactive statistics;
- customer success indicators.

Such improvements should strengthen trust without introducing unnecessary visual complexity.

---

# Status

Approved.

This specification defines the official implementation principles for the Trust Block component within the Premium Avto Design System.
