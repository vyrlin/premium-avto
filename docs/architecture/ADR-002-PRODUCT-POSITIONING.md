# 1. Context

The reverse engineering stage has been completed.

The technical architecture of the legacy system is fully understood and documented.

The project has entered the Product Modernization phase.

At this stage, every UX, design and content decision should reinforce a consistent product identity.

This document defines the official positioning of the Premium Avto brand.

---

# 2. Decision

Premium Avto is positioned as:

> **An independent specialized technical center for the maintenance and repair of VAG Group vehicles.**

Premium Avto is not an official dealership.

Premium Avto is not a general-purpose repair shop.

The company occupies its own position by offering deep specialization, engineering competence and many years of practical experience working with VAG vehicles.

---

# 3. Market Position

Premium Avto does not compete with official dealerships in scale.

Premium Avto does not compete with ordinary repair shops on price.

Premium Avto competes through expertise.

Its competitive advantage is based on:

- deep specialization;
- engineering approach;
- accurate diagnostics;
- complex repairs;
- correct spare parts selection;
- long-term customer trust.

---

# 4. Brand Promise

The official positioning statement is:

> **We do not compete with official dealerships in scale, nor with ordinary repair shops on price. We provide an expert level of maintenance and repair for VAG vehicles, built on deep specialization, many years of experience and an engineering approach to every repair.**

This statement defines the long-term direction of product development.

---

# 5. Core Values

Every public page should reinforce the following values.

- VAG specialization
- Engineering mindset
- Honest diagnostics
- Correct spare parts selection
- Responsibility for results
- Long-term customer trust
- Technical competence
- Continuous professional development

---

# 6. Brand Differentiators

## VAG specialization

Premium Avto specializes in servicing VAG Group vehicles.

Primary brands:

- Audi
- Volkswagen
- Škoda
- SEAT

Additional vehicles accepted by prior agreement:

- Porsche
- Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles

The website should consistently communicate this specialization.

---

## Experience

Premium Avto has operated since 2006.

Several specialists have more than 30 years of experience working specifically with VAG vehicles.

This experience represents accumulated practical knowledge rather than simply years in business.

---

## Expert diagnostics

Diagnostics is one of the company's strongest competencies.

Official communication should emphasize expertise without criticizing competitors.

Preferred wording:

> **We successfully solve faults that other repair shops were unable to resolve.**

---

## Complex repairs

Premium Avto performs complex repairs of engines and major vehicle assemblies using established engineering methods and provides a warranty for completed work.

Confidence should come from experience rather than advertising.

---

## Correct spare parts philosophy

One of the defining principles of Premium Avto is the selection of the correct spare parts.

The company does not recommend the most expensive parts.

The company does not recommend the cheapest parts.

Instead:

> **We recommend not the most expensive and not the cheapest parts, but the right parts for each specific repair.**

This principle should become one of the signature messages of the Premium Avto brand.

---

# 7. Communication Style

Communication should resemble that of a professional engineering organization.

Preferred tone:

- calm;
- confident;
- professional;
- technically competent;
- precise.

Avoid:

- exaggerated marketing language;
- emotional advertising;
- price-oriented messaging;
- unsupported superlatives.

---

# 8. Preferred Terminology

Preferred wording includes:

- Independent Specialized Technical Center
- Specialized VAG Technical Center
- Engineering Approach
- Expert Diagnostics
- Complex Repairs

The phrase "car repair shop" should not be used as the primary description of Premium Avto.

It may remain only where required for SEO purposes.

---

# 9. Design Implications

The visual identity should communicate:

- professionalism;
- precision;
- technical competence;
- cleanliness;
- confidence.

The website should feel closer to premium engineering brands than to traditional repair workshops.

---

# 10. Content Principle

Every new public section should answer one question:

> **Does this strengthen the image of Premium Avto as an independent specialized technical center for VAG vehicles?**

If the answer is no, the content should be reconsidered.

---

# 11. Consequences

This ADR becomes the reference document for:

- Hero
- Trust Block
- Services
- Advantages
- Gallery
- Contacts
- Copywriting
- UX
- Visual Design

Every future public-facing component should remain consistent with this positioning.
